How every business-profile slide opens, on a 1920 × 1080 canvas with 110px side margins.

- **Eyebrow** (`fpd-eyebrow`): a numbered circle, the Thai label, then the English label in small caps, in the `eyebrow` colour (gold-deep on cream, harbour-gold on navy).
- **Headline** (`fpd-title`): Prompt SemiBold 60px (86px on the cover). The second line or phrase goes in `span.g`, which takes `highlight`.
- **English line** (`fpd-en`): DM Serif Display Italic 32px in `highlight`.

The consumer provides the slide number, both labels, the Thai headline split into its two halves, and the English line. Slides alternate navy and cream.
