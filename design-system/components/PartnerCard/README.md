Grower and brand cards on the navy partners band, with the exclusive-partner panel above them.

- **Exclusive partner** (`fp-exclusive`): the V.S.M. Fruit logo on the left, then a badge, a DM Serif Display name and a short text.
- **Grower card** (`fp-partner`): the grower's logo (54px tall, original colours), an origin pill and the varieties in 12.5px.

The consumer provides the logo files. This system shows names in their place, because growers' marks belong to them. Cards stay white even on navy.
