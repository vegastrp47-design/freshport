Small pieces from the business profile.

- **Cover chip** (`fpd-chip`): a gold outline with a solid gold label.
- **Market pills** (`fpd-mkts`): translucent pills with a gold dot, for the markets Fresh Port supplies.
- **Status pill** (`fpd-status`): white with the green `status-open` dot for Open; gold for Coming soon. The word always goes with the dot.
- **Gold badge** (`fpd-badge`): for PROVEN or SIGNATURE.
- **Long-term banner** (`fpd-banner`): the gold gradient bar with navy text.
