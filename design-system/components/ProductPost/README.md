The Instagram product post layout: a 1080 × 1350 studio photo with the corner tab, a two-line headline, a Thai name pill, an origin stamp, a price blob and the LINE pill.

This is a layout reference, not a coded component. Build posts in Canva or Figma, or in HTML as the preview does, from the Graphic elements files. The consumer provides:

- **Photo:** the product photo (light backdrop, navy cloth, pine crate).
- **Headline:** the variety, which becomes line 1 in `midnight-blue`, and the fruit word, which becomes line 2 in its `fruit-*` colour.
- **Thai name:** goes in the pill.
- **Origin:** picks the `origin-stamp-<country>` file.
- **Price and unit:** e.g. `280.-` and `(1 Pack, 500g)`. Add an optional bundle price in a `price-secondary` circle.
- **Colours:** the fruit colour recolours the price blob, and the matching `stripe-*` picks the `corner-tab-<fruit>` file.

Positions, sizes and fonts are in the README's "Instagram product posts" section. Keep every price with its unit, and keep the LINE pill at the bottom centre.
