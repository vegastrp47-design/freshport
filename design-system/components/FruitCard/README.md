The fruit card from the 'On our shelves' grid: a 4:3 photo on `sea-mist`, the English name (Prompt SemiBold 16.5px), the Thai name, and 'Imported from …' with the globe icon.

The consumer provides the photo (square product shots from the Photography group, or an Instagram post with its focus set about 76% down so the headline is hidden), the names, the origin and an optional link. Cards sit four across, then three and two as the page narrows. On hover a card lifts 5px and takes `shadow-md`.
